# Thumbnail research and design workflow

These instructions apply to thumbnail/reference/title-pairing work in this repository, across every topic and project.

1. Read `thumbnail-library/README.md` and `thumbnail-library/asset-index.md` before researching or designing.
2. Identify the actual episode/project and review its current brief and original media. Never apply the gym style to unrelated work.
3. Reuse relevant approved references and licensed assets first. An existing file is not automatically approved.
4. Create/reuse `Thumbnail_References/<Episode_Name>/` and its five numbered folders. Use the templates for new topics.
5. Keep references, final-use assets and private originals separate. This repository is public: do not add personal/private images, raw footage, credentials or third-party artwork without explicit appropriate rights and publication authorization.
6. For research-only requests, finish the reference guide and stop for feedback. Do not render final thumbnails.
7. When final design is authorized, preserve original photographic appearance and actual context. Do not repeatedly regenerate an AI-edited face as if it were an original frame. Reject reconstructed faces, handwriting, equipment, results or expressions.
8. Inspect final outputs at approximately 160×90; verify dimensions and licenses. Never describe visual review as audience testing.
9. Record explicit approvals with scope and date in `thumbnail-library/approved-examples/`. Do not infer approval from generic acknowledgement.
10. Preserve existing files. Report local/remote sync and review limitations honestly.
