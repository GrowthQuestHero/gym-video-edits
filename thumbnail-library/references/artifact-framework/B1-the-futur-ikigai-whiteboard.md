## Useful design lesson
A real board and a large topic word act as visual evidence of a concrete explanation. The physical artifact gives context without requiring a paragraph of overlay text.

## Transfer to the LotusMala case
Keep an authentic 'Buyer Persona' or 'CTA' board detail large; add at most a short 'BUYER → CTA' or '6 STEPS' label.

## Do not transfer
Do not copy the Ikigai content, diagrams, pointing gesture, expertise claim, or career-outcome promise.
