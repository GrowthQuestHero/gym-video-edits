## Reuse conditions
The font licenses permit use/modification/redistribution under their stated conditions. Fonts must not be sold alone; redistributed font files retain their copyright and OFL notices. Check reserved-name conditions before making a modified font. The image produced with a font is not itself forced under OFL. The bundled font files are not modified; they were fetched as base64 and the resulting repository blob SHAs match their source file SHAs. No install/render test was possible while the local environment was offline.

The new collection adds only two font families. No random texture packs, stock portraits or course-brand elements were downloaded. Earlier editable archives may contain other assets, but their font contents could not be inspected offline; do not assume their licenses or approvals.

## Add an asset only when
Its job is needed for a selected direction; the original source, license, filename/version and attribution obligations are recorded; personal/publication status is known; and relevant existing assets have been checked first. A screenshot/reference creator's public image is never automatically a reusable asset.
