# 1% Better with Bibek — reusable thumbnail reference library

Status: **research and preparation; no new final thumbnails created**. Established 2026-10-19.

This library supports **all** of Bibek's videos and projects: fitness, business, marketing, learning/technology, personal growth, products/craft, creative demonstrations and other subjects. It is not a universal gym template.

## Start here on every thumbnail task
1. Read this file, [asset-index.md](asset-index.md), [reference catalog](references/README.md), and [approved examples](approved-examples/README.md).
2. Complete the [episode intake](templates/EPISODE_INTAKE.md) from evidence already supplied. Ask only for missing inputs that affect the core promise or photographic fidelity.
3. Search existing references by **topic AND visual purpose**. Reuse only suitable lessons; research the gaps. A fitness-session reference does not automatically fit a course project.
4. Create or reuse `Thumbnail_References/<Episode_Name>/`: `01_Originals`, `02_Direction_A`, `03_Direction_B`, `04_Direction_C`, `05_Reusable_Assets`.
5. Keep 6–9 useful references per active research collection, organized into three proposed directions. An adjacent course cover or a provisional source must be labeled.
6. Write `REFERENCE_GUIDE.md` with the evidence, critique, reference links, composition lessons, rights, three proposed directions and missing inputs.
7. Present the collection and **wait for feedback when the task is research-only**. Saving this library does not approve a direction or authorize final thumbnail creation.
8. On authorized design work, start from clean original images, preserve their real appearance, pair title and image around one supported promise, and do a mobile-size review.
9. Record the exact approved design/reference, what was approved and where it applies. Reuse that lesson without assuming approval transfers to other topics.

## Topic routes
- **fitness:** session/action/movement; preserve meaningful equipment and actual posture; no fabricated body results.
- **marketing-business:** customer question, a real plan, website, product or board; no invented revenue, clients or campaign proof.
- **learning-tech:** a real project or demonstrated workflow; distinguish personal learning from expert tutorial promises.
- **personal-growth:** a documented event or reflection; no invented emotion, hardship or transformation.
- **product-craft:** a real product, detail or process; verify material/benefit claims.
- **creative-other:** actual performance, demonstration or artifact; do not fake an accomplishment or assume a prior style fits.

The initial curated collection is marketing/learning. Other routes are ready for future intake; they are **not** claimed to have completed subject-specific research.

## Visual-purpose routes
Portrait + question; artifact + framework; personal project; action moment; product detail; before/after **only with authentic, relevant evidence**.

## Rights and privacy
This is a **public repository**. Reference covers are stored as source links and descriptions only. No rights to republish those images or reuse faces/branding are assumed. No personal source images or raw clips were added by this collection. Copyright/licensing of a thumbnail is separate from its creator's popularity.
Font redistribution includes the original copyright/license files. Third-party software licenses are separate from artwork licenses.
